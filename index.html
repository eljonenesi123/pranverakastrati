About CEFTA

The Central European Free Trade Agreement (CEFTA) enhances trade in goods and services, seeks to eliminate trade barriers between its Parties, and attracts investment to the region through fair, stable, and predictable trade rules. CEFTA Parties have committed to establishing a regional economic area aligned with EU standards, aiming to decrease trade and production costs by eliminating market access barriers, with trade in goods and services meant to be free of tariffs, quotas, and non-tariff barriers.

01

Trade in Goods

Removing tariffs, quotas, and non-tariff barriers to goods moving across the region.

02

Trade in Services

Opening regional markets for service providers under predictable, harmonized rules.

03

Cross-Cutting Trade Areas

Covering areas such as trade facilitation, competition, and investment that touch every sector.

04

Dispute Settlement

Providing a binding mechanism to resolve trade disputes between Parties fairly and efficiently.
